Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 6, 2026

Bootstrap Built ("we", "us", "our") operates internal software tools and integrations. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with our applications, including our QuickBooks Online integration.

Information We Collect

Our QuickBooks Online integration accesses financial data from your QuickBooks account solely to provide accounting and bookkeeping functionality. This includes invoices, customers, vendors, accounts, and transactions.

How We Use Information

Data accessed through QuickBooks Online is used exclusively for internal bookkeeping, invoicing, and financial reporting purposes. We do not sell, share, or disclose your financial data to any third parties.

Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to authenticate API requests on your behalf. Financial data is not stored persistently outside of QuickBooks Online.

Third-Party Services

We use the Intuit QuickBooks Online API. Use of QuickBooks Online is subject to Intuit's Privacy Statement.
